01 · About

Consent shouldn't be
a one-time checkbox.

Generative AI raises questions about consent across the AI lifecycle. Before deployment, human-created texts, images, code, music, conversations, and other materials may become training data. After deployment, AI systems can collect information, infer preferences and attributes, retain context, connect to other systems, and act on users' behalf.

This workshop brings together researchers and practitioners to investigate consentful AI as a human-centered approach to the design, development, and governance of AI systems.

We connect consent for the data used to develop AI with consent during human-AI interaction. Rather than treating consent as a one-time checkbox, we investigate it as an ongoing and contextual relationship among people, data, and AI systems.

How might AI systems respect, solicit, represent, negotiate, and operationalize human consent throughout their lifecycle?

02 · Workshop Themes

Questions we want to explore

01

Human–AI Interaction

When should AI explicitly ask permission before remembering, inferring, sharing, or acting?

  • Risk-sensitive confirmation
  • Conversational consent
  • Expiration and revocation
  • Avoiding consent fatigue
02

Agentic AI

How should agents handle the scope of authority when acting toward a user's high-level goal?

  • Actions requiring confirmation
  • Sequences of delegated actions
  • Revisiting authority
  • Conflicting permissions
03

Training Data

What constitutes meaningful consent for using human-created content to train AI?

  • Opt-in and opt-out
  • Granular creator preferences
  • Provenance and documentation
  • Withdrawal after training
04

Design & Evaluation

How do we know whether consent is informed, meaningful, and supported without overwhelming people?

  • Measuring understanding
  • Interaction patterns
  • Dark patterns of AI consent
  • Limits of individual consent

04 · Call for Participation

Join the conversation.

We invite researchers and practitioners from HCI, AI, privacy, responsible AI, design, data governance, machine learning, and related areas.

Submissions may focus on consent in human-AI interaction, consent concerning data used for AI development, or conceptual and technical connections between the two. We particularly encourage concrete consent breakdowns—situations where existing mechanisms for permission, notice, refusal, or withdrawal fail to represent stakeholder intentions.

With author consent, accepted contributions will be published and archived on the workshop website.

Submission format

2–4 pages (excluding references)

Position papers, works-in-progress, design explorations, or provocations, using the ACM Master Article single-column template.

05 · Workshop Activities

A one-day workshop built around making.

Participants will move from mapping problems to analyzing breakdowns, developing design insights, and synthesizing a shared research agenda.

01

Mapping the Consent Landscape

Identify AI consent problems across the lifecycle and build a shared vocabulary for where consent is requested, absent, or inadequate.

02

Consent Breakdown Scenarios

Analyze concrete cases involving data creators, end users, AI agents, bystanders, and withdrawal of previously granted permission.

03

Designing Consentful AI

Turn scenarios into design insights spanning permission dashboards, risk-sensitive confirmation, creator preferences, negotiation, and revocation.

04

Research Agenda

Synthesize open questions across theories of consent, AI agents, dataset governance, creator agency, technical enforcement, evaluation, and the limits of consent.
